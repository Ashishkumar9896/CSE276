## Opening script

I am developing a student project to reduce repetitive customer-support work for a coaching centre. I would like to understand the questions you receive, the current process for bookings and complaints, and when a person must take over. I will not publish personal customer details. You can skip any question or stop the interview at any time.

## Questions

1. Which courses or services do you currently offer?
2. What are the most common questions asked before admission?
3. Which questions consume the most staff time?
4. What are the current fees and payment policies? Who is authorized to verify them?
5. What are the actual batch schedules and how does availability change?
6. How do you currently arrange demo classes or visits?
7. What information do you need before accepting a booking request?
8. What complaints or service issues are common, and how are they resolved?
9. What cases must always be transferred to staff?
10. Which channels do customers use today (in person, calls, website, WhatsApp)?
11. What information should the bot never answer without approval?
12. What would make this project useful to you?
13. How should the system measure success?
14. Who will review FAQ answers and how often should they be updated?

## Post-interview verification

- [ ] Course names verified by owner
- [ ] Fees verified by authorized staff (include effective date)
- [ ] Timings and schedules verified
- [ ] Address/contact details verified
- [ ] Booking and cancellation rules verified
- [ ] Complaint escalation contact and expected response process verified
- [ ] Owner-approved FAQ reviewed before deployment
